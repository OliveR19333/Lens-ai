# Playbook — Hybrid Drone + AI Tour (the premium tier)

The differentiated top-shelf product in [[tnc-offer]]: REAL aerial footage
(Avata FPV flythrough, Mini 4 Pro orbits, shot D-log) fused with Higgsfield
AI interior clips into one cinematic tour. Competitors running AI-only
pipelines cannot match the real aerials — this is the moat.

## Tiers (price accordingly)
1. **AI Tour** — [[re-walkthrough-pipeline]] as-is. No site visit. Baseline.
2. **Hybrid Tour** — this playbook. One drone session + AI interiors.
   Premium price: the aerials are real and it shows.
3. **Hybrid + Shorts pack** — tier 2 plus 3 captioned 9:16 cuts for the
   agent's own social. Near-zero extra cost via `/tnc-video-factory`.

## Shoot day (the only manual part)
- FPV approach: street → over roof → reveal backyard (one continuous take,
  3–5 attempts, keep the best)
- Mini 4 Pro: slow 180° orbit + top-down lot shot, D-log, 4K30
- Grab exterior stills for the proposal portal before/afters

## Post (all on the rig, via /tnc-video-factory)
1. Scrape listing + photos (Apify) → AI interior clips (Higgsfield, unlimited window when active)
2. LUT the D-log, normalize everything to 1080p30
3. Assemble: FPV approach → entry → AI room flow → hero feature → closing orbit
4. Shorts pack: 3 vertical cuts with captions
5. Deliver via the proposal portal — tour as line item, shorts pack as
   toggleable add-on with its own price
